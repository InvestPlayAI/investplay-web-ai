## 2. Startups.com.br — November/2025

**Title:** "With its own AI, InvestPlay wants to 'change the game' in banking data"

**Outlet:** Startups.com.br

**Link:** https://startups.com.br/negocios/com-ia-propria-investplay-quer-mudar-o-jogo-dos-dados-bancarios/

**Coverage highlights:**
- InvestPlay developed a proprietary foundation model specialized in transactional data, capable of transforming financial data into intelligent segmentation and offer hyper-personalization
- The model operates with AutoML 24 hours a day, continuously learning and combining variables without the need for manual rules
- Partnership established with Databricks — a platform present in 95% of Brazilian banks — enabling native integration within the multinational's ecosystem
- Institution data remains under internal control, with no dependency on public models such as GPTs, ensuring governance and security

**Quote from CEO Iago Oselieri:** "While traditional machine learning depends on manually created rules, our model learns and combines variables 24 hours a day."

## 5. Let's Money Podcast — October/2025

**Title:** "Hyper-personalization and the power of data"

**Outlet:** Let's Money Podcast

**Guests:** Iago Oselieri (founder and CEO of InvestPlay) and Gabriel Pereira

**YouTube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1v3d_8vcZ4M

**Coverage highlights:**
- Insighter runs natively on Databricks infrastructure, used by approximately 90% of Brazilian banks, without creating parallel pipelines
- The proprietary AI was trained exclusively on banking transactional data, creating embeddings capable of interpreting consumption patterns and behavior in real time
- From millions of tax IDs (CPFs), the AI generates an average of 6 actionable insights per customer, ready for activation in campaigns, products, and banking relationships
- The model identifies non-obvious correlations — such as pet-related spending linked to card churn — that would go unnoticed by generic models
- All operations occur within the bank's environment, ensuring no data leaves the institution

**Quote from CEO Iago Oselieri:** "I'm not going to create a new pipeline. I'm going straight to the water tank and running through the existing pipes, reaching the same faucet — but with richer data."

**Future vision:** "The next wave will be autonomous agents, capable of making contextual decisions based on data."
