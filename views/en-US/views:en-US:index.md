## 1. What is InvestPlay

InvestPlay is a Brazilian financial data intelligence platform that transforms transactional data into real business opportunities for financial institutions. Through proprietary AI models, the company delivers hyper-personalization, behavioral segmentation, and personal financial management for banks, credit unions, and fintechs.

**Value proposition:** "Reveal behaviors, anticipate needs, and activate hyper-personalized banking relationship journeys with AI, at the most relevant moment in the customer's life."

**Tagline:** Brazil's first conversational intelligence for hyper-personalization and segmentation of financial data.

**CNPJ (Tax ID):** 36.535.941/0001-77

**Offices:**
- Av. Bias Fortes, 382 - 7th floor - Lourdes, Belo Horizonte - MG, 30170-011
- R. da Consolação, 2302 - Consolação, São Paulo - SP, 01301-000

### Who it serves

InvestPlay primarily serves:
- Banks and credit unions seeking to unlock transactional data value
- Fintechs focused on offer hyper-personalization
- Financial institutions looking to reduce data team backlog in analytics
- CRM, credit, cards, investments, and insurance professionals

**Clients include:** Sicredi, Unicred, Banrisul, B3, BTG Pactual, BRB, among others.

### InvestPlay is not a bank

InvestPlay is an intelligence layer on top of financial data. It does not move funds or operate as a financial institution — it connects transactional data (internal and Open Finance) to AI models to generate actionable insights.

## 3. Solutions

### 3.1 Insighter

**What it is:** Hyper-personalization platform with AI and 200+ ready-to-use business use cases.

**Who it's for:** Financial institutions that want to turn transactional data into use cases within days, without competing for technical team bandwidth.

**How it works:**

- **On-demand analytics (no-code & interactive):** Explore 220+ standardized insights for card spending increase, investment allocation, credit portability, earned income, payment capacity, and more.
- **Custom cross-analysis creation:** Configure pipelines or create your own use cases with a few clicks, no data team dependency.
- **Simplified export & integration:** Native Databricks solution, compatible with AWS, GCP, and Azure; integrates with CRMs like Salesforce, Adobe, and Insider.
- **Proprietary AI vertical:** AI models compute behavioral representations of customers, generating embeddings that continuously interpret and monitor behavioral events in real time.

**Allyra:** The conversational AI feature of Insighter that simplifies financial data analysis and creates hyper-personalized banking experiences with autonomy, precision, and speed.

**Measured benefits:**
- -50% customer acquisition costs (Source: McKinsey)
- +71% customer retention (Source: Zendesk)
- +15x actionable segments identified with AI (Source: Salesforce)

**Use cases covered:** CRM, Credit, Cards, Investments, Insurance, Beyond Banking, Data Intelligence.

### 3.2 Facetas (Audiences)

**What it is:** Intelligent audience segmentation solution via advanced behavioral clusters, generating 300+ contextualized facets.

**Who it's for:** Professionals and institutions that need to build lookalike and propensity models, hyper-segmented campaigns, and life-moment-based decisions — without code, spreadsheets, or waiting for the data team.

**How it works:**

- **Automated and scalable segmentation:** Audience segmented in real time by 300+ facets of interests, demographics, financial products, and life moments.
- **Advanced clustering in a few clicks:** Combine financial behavior variables and consumption habits beyond standard segmentations.
- **Actionable profiles:** Reach profiles such as "financially cautious", "novelty lovers", "gig workers", and more.
- **Lookalike and propensity models with AI:** Computational behavioral representations of customers via embeddings for lookalike and propensity models.

**Measured benefits:**
- +15% revenue with segmentation and personalization (Source: McKinsey)
- +20% conversion with personalization (Source: McKinsey)
- +8x ROI with hyper-personalized marketing (Source: Deloitte)

### 3.3 Personal Financial Manager (PFM)

**What it is:** White-label personal financial management solution for end clients, generating behavioral and business insights for the institution.

**Who it's for:** Financial institutions that want to deepen client relationships, deliver relevant experiences, and enhance their mobile banking with account aggregation, cards, and spending management.

**How it works:**

- **Financial management beyond the statement:** Clients visualize and control their entire financial life in one place, with personalized alerts and recommendations.
- **360° view and applied intelligence:** Internal data + Open Finance enriches the client view; activates smarter up-selling and cross-selling strategies with contextualized offers.
- **Retention and primacy:** Clients recognize value in the relationship, increasing trust and openness to new solutions.
- **AI layer for transaction labeling:** AI algorithms automatically categorize transactions, providing clients with a clear view of their financial life.
- **100% white-label widgets:** Components via SDK and Webview, fully integrable into the institution's design system (mobile).

**Measured benefits:**
- 9% churn among PFM users, vs. 10–15% sector average (Source: MX.com)
- +50% logins per user (Source: MX.com)
- +15% deposits when PFM is incorporated (Source: MX.com)

## 8. Glossary of Key Terms

| Term | Definition in InvestPlay Context |
|------|----------------------------------|
| Hyper-personalization | Delivery of individually tailored offers, journeys, and experiences based on actual customer financial behavior |
| Open Finance | Brazilian system for data sharing with customer consent, regulated by the Central Bank of Brazil |
| Primacy | The position of preferred bank/institution in a customer's financial life |
| Behavioral embedding | Vectorial computational representation of a customer's financial behavior, generated by proprietary AI models |
| Faceta | Dimensional segmentation of a customer (e.g., "gig worker", "financially cautious") |
| NBO/NBA | Next Best Offer / Next Best Action — strategies for delivering the most relevant next action to the customer |
| Lookalike | Model that identifies new customers with profiles similar to the best current customers |
| Transaction labeling | Automatic AI categorization of financial transactions |
| Life moment | Customer's current personal and financial context (e.g., home purchase, childbirth, job change) |
